## Resolved

| # | Apparent conflict | Ruling |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Levels must be hit after 9:30" (board) vs "just because this level got hit pre-market doesn't matter" `[1:37]` | **Both are true, for two different models.** Core model: level hit after 9:30. Scalping model: a pre-market hit is the framework, and the board labels it exactly that |
| 2 | "Only levels not traded into" `[1:33]` vs trading off a PDH already hit `[1:37]` | Unhit levels are your **targets and core-model levels**. A level already hit can still give direction for the scalping model |
| 3 | Minimum R: "down to 1.5" `[1:38]` and board "above 1.5, should be higher" vs slide "Liquidity at 2R Minimum" `[1:16]` | **Hard floor is above 1.5R.** Aim for 2R or more. Jesse's own 1.73R trade on Feb 21 confirms the floor |
| 4 | Stop "almost always at candle bodies" `[1:37]` vs "just beyond the high or low" (board) | **Depends on trade location.** First reaction at a level: beyond the high/low. Between levels: bodies/SFP |
| 5 | Entry timeframe 1M `[1:34]` vs "1M or 5M" (board) | Both valid. **M15 levels → M1, H1 levels → M5**, following the alignment table |
| 6 | Trail only on manipulated swings `[1:50]` vs trail every swing `[1:52]` | **Two phases.** Before the target: manipulated swings only. At the target (price weighting): every new swing |
| 7 | "Never have a bias" vs "you can lean bullish" `[1:08]` and "you can only look at one side when aligned" `[0:49]` | A lean or alignment **adjusts size and confidence**. It never stops you taking the other side once the market flips |
| 8 | "Almost 70% win rate" `[1:19]` vs "I don't have a 70% win rate, usually 55–65" `[1:26]` | ~68% is the **no-bias subset** of a 400-trade sample. **55–65% is the long-run figure** |

| 11 | $46,000 "in the last couple of weeks" `[0:00]` vs "in a single month" (slide `[1:31]`) vs the statement dates Feb 14 – Apr 14 2025 `[0:01]` | The broker statement is the record: **+$46,522.44 from Feb 14 to Apr 14 2025** |
