# The All-In-One ICT Trading Guide

Built from **"Easiest Way To Trade ICT in 2026 as a Beginner"** by Jesse Rogers (Casper Trading), 2:08:25.
Every rule carries a video timestamp like `[1:36]` so it can be re-watched. The method is presented here
as Jesse teaches it: a simple, repeatable system that has produced real payouts for Jesse and for the traders
Jesse mentors.

## The whole method in three lines

1. **Everything is key levels + confirmation.** Where do you do business, and what must you see there? `[0:34]`
2. **Time picks the levels.** Time-based liquidity turns subjective chart reading into a mechanical list. `[0:40]`
3. **React, never predict.** A level either reverses or flips, and both pay when you have a plan for each. `[1:11]`
